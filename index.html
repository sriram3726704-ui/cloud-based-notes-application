Firebase Cloud Setup

Cloud Storage with Firebase Firestore:
Configure your Firebase credentials below to enable real-time cloud synchronization. Your notes will be safely stored in Firestore and update across all your devices in real-time.

Step 1: Go to Firebase Console and click Create a project.

Step 2: In the left menu, click Build > Firestore Database, then click Create database. Choose Start in test mode (for development access).

Step 3: Go to Project Settings (gear icon), scroll down to Your apps, click the Web (</>) icon, and copy the firebaseConfig object.

Step 4: Paste it in the Paste Firebase Config tab or edit firebase-config.js directly in your code!
